## Conference Overview

We are delighted to announce that **Osaka, Japan** will host the 2027 AMA Global Marketing SIG Conference — **the first time this prestigious conference will be held in Japan**. This landmark event is expected to welcome approximately **170 participants**, including around 150 international attendees, bringing together leading scholars, practitioners, and doctoral students from across the globe to advance international marketing research and education.

Join us for an exhilarating academic program, immersive cultural experiences, and stimulating conversations in one of Asia's most dynamic business hubs.

## Host Institution

### Ritsumeikan University

*Freedom and innovation, the spirit of Ritsumeikan.*

Ritsumeikan University traces its roots to a private academy — **"Ritsumeikan" (立命館)** — established by Prince Saionji Kinmochi in 1869 within his residence in the Kyoto Imperial Garden, and formally founded as a higher education institution by Kojuro Nakagawa, a secretary of Saionji, in 1900.

Today Ritsumeikan is **the largest university in western Japan**, with main campuses in Kyoto, Osaka, and Shiga. It serves more than **36,000 students** and **3,500 faculty members**, and was ranked **4th among private universities in Japan** in the 2023 QS World University Rankings. Embodying its founding principles of *"freedom and innovation,"* Ritsumeikan is widely recognized as one of Japan's most advanced and innovative universities.

## Conference Venue

### Future Plaza, OIC Campus

The conference will be held at **Future Plaza** on the **Osaka Ibaraki Campus (OIC)** of Ritsumeikan University — a symbol of the university's commitment to regional and social collaboration, established through public-private cooperation with Ibaraki City.

The OIC Campus is developed as a *"campus open to the community."* As a joint public-private redevelopment project, the former beer factory site was integrated into a disaster-prevention park surrounded by university facilities in an L-shaped layout — bringing higher education and civic life together on the same site.

## Getting There

### Kansai International Airport (KIX)

Getting to Japan is straightforward thanks to extensive international flight service. **Kansai International Airport (KIX)** handles more than **1,200 international flights weekly** from over **60 cities** across roughly **20 countries** on **80+ airlines** — making Osaka especially well-connected for global participants.

### To OIC Campus

The OIC Campus sits within walking distance of multiple railway stations served by **JR**, **Hankyu Railway**, and the **Osaka Monorail**. From KIX you can travel directly to the campus area, and the **Shinkansen** (bullet train) and domestic flights make it easy to combine the conference with travel elsewhere in Japan before or after.

## About Osaka

Osaka is **the largest city in Western Japan** — home to roughly **2.75 million residents** (World Population Review, 2023) — and Japan's third-largest city overall. It is a dynamic blend of modern innovation and traditional culture, known affectionately as **"the nation's kitchen"** (*tenka no daidokoro*) for its exceptional culinary scene, with world-class dining, historic temples and castles, and a welcoming, entrepreneurial spirit.

As a major commercial center serving as headquarters for leading multinational corporations and innovative startups, the city's business ecosystem provides an ideal backdrop for exploring contemporary issues in international marketing, particularly in the **Asia-Pacific context**.

In *The Economist Intelligence Unit's* **Safe Cities Index 2021**, Osaka ranked as the **17th safest city in the world**, with above-average scores across every category — and **a top-five ranking in health security**. The city has also placed in the top ten of the EIU's **Global Liveability Index** every year since 2018, with strong scores for stability, education, healthcare, and infrastructure.
