## Conference Overview

We are delighted to announce that **Osaka, Japan** will host the 2027 AMA Global Marketing SIG Conference — **the first time this conference will be held in Japan**. The conference will take place at **Future Plaza, Ritsumeikan University's Osaka Ibaraki Campus (OIC)** from **May 20–23, 2027**, and is expected to welcome approximately **170 participants**, including around 150 international attendees.

Join us for a rigorous academic program — competitive sessions, a doctoral consortium, and a *Journal of International Marketing* special issue session — paired with the rich culture and hospitality of the Kansai region.

## Host Institution

*Freedom and innovation, the spirit of Ritsumeikan.*

Ritsumeikan University traces its roots to a private academy — **"Ritsumeikan" (立命館)** — established by Prince Saionji Kinmochi in 1869 within his residence in the Kyoto Imperial Garden, and formally founded as a higher education institution by Kojuro Nakagawa in 1900.

Today Ritsumeikan is **the largest university in western Japan**, with main campuses in Kyoto, Osaka, and Shiga. It serves more than **36,000 students** and **3,500 faculty members**, and was ranked **4th among private universities in Japan** in the 2023 QS World University Rankings.

The conference will be held at **Future Plaza** on the **Osaka Ibaraki Campus (OIC)** of Ritsumeikan University — a symbol of the university's commitment to regional and social collaboration, established through public-private cooperation with Ibaraki City. ## About Osaka

Osaka is **the largest city in Western Japan** — home to roughly **2.75 million residents** (World Population Review, 2023) — and Japan's third-largest city overall. Known affectionately as **"the nation's kitchen"** (*tenka no daidokoro*) for its exceptional culinary scene, Osaka is a dynamic blend of modern innovation and traditional culture, with world-class dining, historic temples and castles, and a welcoming, entrepreneurial spirit. As a major commercial center serving as headquarters for leading multinationals and innovative startups, the city provides an ideal backdrop for exploring contemporary issues in international marketing — particularly in the **Asia-Pacific context**.

In *The Economist Intelligence Unit's* **Safe Cities Index 2021**, Osaka ranked as the **17th safest city in the world**, with a **top-five score in health security**. The city has also placed in the top ten of the EIU's **Global Liveability Index** every year since 2018.

## Travel & Stay

### Getting There

Osaka is exceptionally well-connected. **Kansai International Airport (KIX)** handles more than **1,200 international flights weekly** from over **60 cities** in around 20 countries on 80+ airlines, reaching central Osaka in roughly 50–60 minutes. From Tokyo, the **Shinkansen** reaches Osaka in 2.5 hours; from Kyoto, just 15 minutes. The OIC Campus sits within walking distance of multiple stations served by **JR**, **Hankyu Railway**, and the **Osaka Monorail**.

**Visa requirements** vary by nationality. Allow 6–8 weeks for processing if needed; the conference can provide visa support letters on request.

## Optional Excursions

Osaka is the travel hub of the Kansai region. Major destinations such as **Kyoto** and **Kobe** are just a short train ride away, so participants can use the conference as a base for exploring this fascinating part of Japan — rich in culture, history, nature, and city life.

<Callout title="Osaka — CUPNOODLES Museum">

The instant noodles you see in supermarkets worldwide were invented here. **Dr. Momofuku Ando**, founder of Nissin Foods and a **Ritsumeikan University alumnus**, created **Chicken Ramen** — the world's first instant noodles — in 1958 to help post-war Japan recover from food shortages. The CUPNOODLES Museum tells the full story and lets visitors design their own custom cup.

</Callout>

<Callout title="Kyoto — Japanese Potted Gardening">

A hands-on cultural experience guided by gardeners who maintain some of Kyoto's most famous gardens. Tour garden masterpieces, learn about the spirit and history of Japanese gardens, then collaborate in small groups to design and craft a **potted garden of your own** — a memorable team-building experience that opens a window into the depth of Japanese culture.

</Callout>

<Callout title="Kobe — Takenaka Carpentry Tools Museum">

Two stars in the *Michelin Green Guide Japan* and ranked **#1 on TripAdvisor for Kobe (2019)**, this is the only museum of its kind in Japan. Explore around **1,000 carefully selected tools and creations** drawn from a collection of more than **35,000 pieces**, with a focus on the traditional **Miya** and **Sukiya** carpentry behind Japan's shrines, temples, and castles.

</Callout>

## Submission & Review

Papers and extended abstracts will be submitted electronically through the **EasyChair** conference system, expected to open in Fall 2026. Each manuscript may be submitted to **only one track** and is reviewed through **blind peer review** by program committee members; acceptance decisions are communicated several months before the conference. Submissions may take the form of full papers (max 40 double-spaced pages), extended abstracts for advanced work-in-progress (max 10 pages), or special-session proposals.
