## 1994–2010 Conferences

From 1994–2010, the SIG organized conferences and special sessions through partnerships with the **Academy of Marketing (UK)** and in conjunction with **AMA Educators' Conferences**. Conference locations during this period included venues in Europe, Latin America, and Asia.
