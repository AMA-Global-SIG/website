## 1994–2010 Conferences

From 1994–2010, the SIG organized conferences and special sessions through partnerships with the **Academy of Marketing (UK)** and in conjunction with **AMA Educators' Conferences**. Conference locations during this period included venues in Europe, Latin America, and Asia.

<div class="bg-blue-50 dark:bg-slate-800 p-5 rounded-lg my-6">

**Founding Era Highlights**

- Bi-annual joint conferences with Academy of Marketing (UK) established
- Expansion to international venues outside the United States
- Partnership model developed for conference organization
- Complete records from this founding period are being compiled

</div>
