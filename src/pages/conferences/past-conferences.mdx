## The Early Years (1994–2010)

From 1994–2010, the SIG organized conferences and special sessions through partnerships with the **Academy of Marketing (UK)** and in conjunction with **AMA Educators' Conferences**. The bi-annual joint conferences with the Academy of Marketing established a durable partnership model for conference organization, and venues progressively expanded beyond the United States to international locations across Europe, Latin America, and Asia.
