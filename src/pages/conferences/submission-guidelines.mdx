### Competitive Sessions

Two types of submissions are accepted for competitive sessions:

<div class="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 my-6">
  <div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 p-5 rounded-lg border border-brand-100 dark:border-brand-700">
    <h4 class="font-bold mb-2">Full Paper Submissions</h4>
    <p class="text-sm">
      Complete manuscripts of no more than <strong>40 double-spaced pages</strong>, inclusive of all materials
      (abstract, text, footnotes, references, tables, figures, and appendices).
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 p-5 rounded-lg border border-brand-100 dark:border-brand-700">
    <h4 class="font-bold mb-2">Extended Abstracts</h4>
    <p class="text-sm">
      Shorter submissions (<strong>maximum 10 double-spaced pages</strong>) focusing on projects that are sufficiently
      advanced. Should clearly articulate research question, theoretical contribution, methodology, and expected
      findings.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 border-l-4 border-brand-400 p-4 rounded-r-lg my-6">

**Note:** Submitting a manuscript as a competitive paper does not guarantee placement in a competitive session. The best papers in each track will be placed in competitive sessions, while other accepted papers may be placed in interactive sessions due to space considerations.

</div>

### Interactive Sessions

Shorter manuscripts or work-in-progress pieces that could benefit from informal feedback are appropriate for interactive sessions. These sessions are held in poster-style or roundtable discussion format.

- Interactive submissions should be extended abstracts of no more than **10 double-spaced pages**

### Special Session Proposals

Special session proposals should include:

- A short introduction framing the session theme and its relevance to international marketing
- Three to four extended abstracts (approximately 3-4 pages each)
- A list of participants with their expertise and institutional affiliations
- Contact information for the session organizer(s)

> Proposals are assessed based on scholarly contribution, thematic coherence, and potential to generate interest and discussion.

## Formatting Requirements

| Element                      | Requirement                                                |
| :--------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Language**                 | English                                                    |
| **File Format**              | Microsoft Word (.doc, .docx) or PDF                        |
| **Font**                     | 12-point Times New Roman                                   |
| **Spacing**                  | Double-spaced (tables and references may be single-spaced) |
| **Margins**                  | 1 inch (2.54 cm) on all sides                              |
| **Page Layout**              | No page numbers, line numbers, or headers/footers          |
| **Citation Style**           | APA style (or consistent with AMA journal conventions)     |
| **Full Paper Length**        | Maximum 40 pages (all materials included)                  |
| **Extended Abstract Length** | Maximum 10 pages (all materials included)                  |

## Anonymization Requirements

All submissions undergo **blind review**. To ensure anonymity:

<div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 border-l-4 border-brand-400 p-5 rounded-r-lg my-6">

**Anonymization Checklist:**

- Remove all author names, affiliations, and contact information from the manuscript
- Anonymize self-citations (e.g., replace _"As we showed in Author (2023)..."_ with _"As shown in [Author] (2023)..."_)
- Remove or anonymize acknowledgments, funding sources, and institutional references
- Remove author-identifying metadata from file properties
  - In Word: `File > Info > Check for Issues > Inspect Document`

</div>
