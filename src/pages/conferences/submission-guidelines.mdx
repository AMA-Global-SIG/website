These guidelines apply to all paper submissions to the AMA Global Marketing SIG Annual Conference. Please read carefully before submitting your work through the **EasyChair** conference management system.

---

## Submission Types

### Competitive Sessions

Two types of submissions are accepted for competitive sessions:

- **Full Paper Submissions** — complete manuscripts of no more than **40 double-spaced pages**, inclusive of all materials (abstract, text, footnotes, references, tables, figures, and appendices).
- **Extended Abstracts** — shorter submissions of **maximum 10 double-spaced pages**, focusing on projects that are sufficiently advanced. Should clearly articulate the research question, theoretical contribution, methodology, and expected findings.

<Callout tone="muted">

**Note:** Submitting a manuscript as a competitive paper does not guarantee placement in a competitive session. The best papers in each track will be placed in competitive sessions, while other accepted papers may be placed in interactive sessions due to space considerations.

</Callout>

### Interactive Sessions

Shorter manuscripts or **work-in-progress** pieces that could benefit from informal feedback are appropriate for interactive sessions. These are held in **poster-style** format, and submissions should be **extended abstracts of no more than 10 double-spaced pages**.

### Special Session Proposals

Special session proposals should include a **short introduction** framing the session theme and its relevance to international marketing, **three to four extended abstracts**, a **list of participants** with their institutional affiliations, and **contact information** for the session organizer(s). Proposals are assessed based on scholarly contribution, thematic coherence, and potential to generate interest and discussion.

## Formatting Requirements

| Element | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Language** | English |
| **File Format** | Microsoft Word (.doc, .docx) or PDF |
| **Font** | 12-point Times New Roman |
| **Spacing** | Double-spaced (tables and references may be single-spaced) |
| **Margins** | 1 inch (2.54 cm) on all sides |
| **Page Layout** | No page numbers, line numbers, or headers/footers |
| **Citation Style** | APA style (or consistent with AMA journal conventions) |
| **Full Paper Length** | Maximum 40 pages (all materials included) |
| **Extended Abstract Length** | Maximum 10 pages (all materials included) |

---

## Anonymization Requirements

All submissions undergo **blind review**. To ensure anonymity:

<Callout title="Anonymization Checklist">

- Remove all author names, affiliations, and contact information from the manuscript
- Anonymize self-citations (e.g., replace *"As we showed in Author (2023)..."* with *"As shown in [Author] (2023)..."*)
- Remove or anonymize acknowledgments, funding sources, and institutional references
- Remove author-identifying metadata from file properties
  - In Word: `File > Info > Check for Issues > Inspect Document`

</Callout>

## Submission Rules

- **Single track submission** — each paper may only be submitted to one track. Select the track that best fits your research topic.
- **Original work** — submitted papers must not have been published or accepted for publication elsewhere.
- **Concurrent journal submissions** — papers under review at journals may be submitted, provided they do not appear in print or online before the conference.
- **Post-submission changes** — substantive changes to title, abstract, authorship, or content are not permitted after the deadline. Minor corrections are allowed during the camera-ready phase.
- **Presentation requirement** — at least one author of each accepted paper must register and attend to present. Papers without a registered presenting author may be withdrawn.

---

## Review Process

All competitive manuscript submissions undergo blind review. Submissions are evaluated based on:

- **Relevance** to the international marketing community
- **Contribution** to research, teaching, and/or practice
- **Conceptual and analytical rigor**
- **Appropriateness of methodology**
- **Innovativeness** of approach or findings
- **Significance and clarity** of conclusions

---

## After Acceptance

Authors of accepted papers will receive:

<Callout title="What happens next">

1. **Session Assignment** — Notification of competitive or interactive placement
2. **Registration Deadline** — Conference logistics and registration information
3. **Presentation Details** — Guidelines and schedule (closer to conference date)

</Callout>

---

## Tips for a Successful Submission

Submit well before the deadline to avoid last-minute technical issues. After uploading, download and review your submission to verify formatting, and retain your EasyChair submission ID for future correspondence. If you encounter technical difficulties, contact the track chairs or program chairs promptly.
