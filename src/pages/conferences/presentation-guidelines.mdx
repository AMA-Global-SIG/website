**Congratulations on having your work accepted.** These guidelines will help you deliver an effective presentation that engages the audience and maximizes your research contribution.

## Competitive Paper Presentations

### Session Format

Competitive sessions are 75 minutes with typically four papers. The session chair opens with a 5-minute introduction of the theme and papers. Each presenter has 10–12 minutes, followed by a few questions from the audience. The session closes with 10 minutes of general Q&A. Session chairs enforce time limits to ensure fair treatment of all presenters.

### Before Your Session

Bring your presentation as a PowerPoint (.pptx) or PDF on a USB drive, and email yourself a backup in case of technical issues. The conference provides a projector and computer; if you have special equipment needs, confirm them in advance with the program chairs. Arrive 10 minutes early to test equipment, load your presentation, introduce yourself to the chair and co-presenters, confirm the presentation order, and distribute any handouts. If you cannot attend, notify your session chair immediately.

### Handling Q&A

- Listen carefully; repeat questions if needed for the room to hear
- Acknowledge good points even when they challenge your work
- If you don't know an answer, say so and suggest how you would investigate it
- Invite questioners to continue the conversation afterward
- Keep responses concise to allow multiple questions

## Interactive (Poster) Presentations

Interactive sessions provide in-depth, informal feedback on work-in-progress through poster format. Senior scholars attend specifically to provide feedback.

### Poster Specifications

| Specification | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Maximum size** | A0 (841×1189 mm / 33×47 inches) |
| **Orientation** | Portrait (not landscape) |
| **Font size** | Readable from 1+ meters (body 24+ pt, headings 36+ pt) |
| **Language** | English only |
| **Required elements** | Title, authors, affiliations, contact email for lead author |

### During Your Poster Session

- **Prepare to facilitate discussion:** Bring business cards, paper, sticky notes, and markers
- **Stay at your poster** for the entire session
- **Engage visitors proactively:** *"Would you like me to walk you through it?"*
- **Be ready to discuss** limitations, alternative approaches, and next steps
- **If few people visit:** Use the time to visit other posters, build connections, and observe effective designs
