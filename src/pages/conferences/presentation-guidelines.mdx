## Competitive Paper Presentations

### Session Format

<div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 p-5 rounded-lg my-6">

**Competitive sessions are 75 minutes** with typically 4 papers:

| Time | Activity |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2 min | Session chair introduces theme and papers |
| 60 min | Four 15-minute presentations |
| 13 min | Audience Q&A |

**Your presentation time is 15 minutes.** Session chairs will enforce time limits to ensure fair treatment of all presenters and adequate discussion time.

</div>

### Before Your Session

Arrive 10 minutes early to:

- Test equipment and load your presentation
- Introduce yourself to the chair and co-presenters
- Confirm presentation order
- Distribute any handouts

> *If you cannot attend, notify your session chair immediately.*

### Technical Requirements

| Category | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **File Format** | PowerPoint (.pptx) or PDF. Bring on USB drive. Email yourself a backup. |
| **Fonts** | Arial or Times New Roman only. Minimum 24-point size. |
| **Colors** | High contrast for readability. Avoid red-green combinations. |
| **Equipment** | Projector and computer provided. Confirm special needs in advance. |

### Presentation Best Practices

#### Structure Your Talk

<div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 p-5 rounded-lg my-6">

**Start strong:**

- Lead with your main finding or contribution
- Use a real-world example to illustrate the problem
- State clearly what is **new** about your work

**For each section:**

- **Theory:** What is the problem? Why does it matter? What do you add?
- **Methods:** Briefly justify your approach and establish face validity
- **Results:** Report direction of effects and what the data tell you—not statistical tests
- **Conclusion:** What have we learned? What should be done next?

</div>

#### Effective Delivery

<div class="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 my-6">
  <div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 p-5 rounded-lg border border-brand-100 dark:border-brand-700">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-green-700 dark:text-green-400 mb-3">✓ DO</h4>
    <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm">
      <li>✅ <strong>Talk to the audience</strong>, not at them—make eye contact</li>
      <li>✅ <strong>Describe your contribution</strong>; urge them to read the paper for details</li>
      <li>✅ <strong>Pause after key points</strong> to let ideas land</li>
      <li>✅ <strong>Acknowledge limitations</strong> candidly</li>
      <li>✅ <strong>Plan for 13 minutes</strong> of content—easier to elaborate than cut</li>
      <li>✅ <strong>Identify where you want feedback</strong> and ask for it</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 p-5 rounded-lg border border-brand-100 dark:border-brand-700">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-red-700 dark:text-red-400 mb-3">✗ DON'T</h4>
    <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm">
      <li>❌ <strong>Read from slides</strong> or provide comprehensive literature review</li>
      <li>❌ <strong>Explain every detail</strong> of your methodology</li>
      <li>❌ <strong>Describe instrument validation</strong> or sample characteristics</li>
      <li>❌ <strong>Save your conclusion</strong> as a surprise ending</li>
      <li>❌ <strong>Ignore time warnings</strong> from the chair</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

#### Slide Design

<div class="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 my-6">
  <div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 p-5 rounded-lg border border-brand-100 dark:border-brand-700">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-green-700 dark:text-green-400 mb-3">✓ DO</h4>
    <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm">
      <li>✅ One key idea per slide</li>
      <li>✅ Minimal text—slides support your talk, they do not replace it</li>
      <li>✅ Simple, clear figures and tables</li>
      <li>✅ Large fonts (28+ point for body text)</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 p-5 rounded-lg border border-brand-100 dark:border-brand-700">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-red-700 dark:text-red-400 mb-3">✗ DON'T</h4>
    <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm">
      <li>❌ Cram multiple messages onto one slide</li>
      <li>❌ Use slides with tiny fonts or dense tables</li>
      <li>❌ Include every detail from your paper</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

#### Handling Q&A

- Listen carefully; repeat questions if needed for the room to hear
- Acknowledge good points even when they challenge your work
- If you don't know an answer, say so and suggest how you would investigate it
- Invite questioners to continue the conversation afterward
- Keep responses concise to allow multiple questions

## Interactive (Poster) Presentations

Interactive sessions provide in-depth, informal feedback on work-in-progress through poster or roundtable format. Senior scholars attend specifically to provide feedback.

### Poster Specifications

| Specification | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Maximum size** | A0 (841×1189 mm / 33×47 inches) |
| **Orientation** | Portrait (not landscape) |
| **Font size** | Readable from 1+ meters (body 24+ pt, headings 36+ pt) |
| **Language** | English only |
| **Required elements** | Title, authors, affiliations, contact email for lead author |

### Effective Poster Design

<div class="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 my-6">
  <div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 p-5 rounded-lg border border-brand-100 dark:border-brand-700">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-green-700 dark:text-green-400 mb-3">✓ DO</h4>
    <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm">
      <li>✅ <strong>Structure clearly:</strong> Problem → Method → Key Finding → Implication</li>
      <li>✅ <strong>Lead with visuals:</strong> Use figures, charts, and diagrams</li>
      <li>✅ <strong>Limit text:</strong> Use bullet points; avoid dense paragraphs</li>
      <li>✅ <strong>Create visual hierarchy:</strong> Most important elements immediately visible</li>
      <li>✅ <strong>Include a "takeaway box":</strong> 2-3 sentences summarizing contribution</li>
      <li>✅ <strong>Prepare a 5-minute overview:</strong> Most visitors want a brief guided tour</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 p-5 rounded-lg border border-brand-100 dark:border-brand-700">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-red-700 dark:text-red-400 mb-3">✗ DON'T</h4>
    <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm">
      <li>❌ <strong>Reproduce your paper on a poster</strong>—posters are visual summaries</li>
      <li>❌ <strong>Use tiny fonts</strong> or dense text blocks</li>
      <li>❌ <strong>Show every analysis</strong>—focus on core findings</li>
      <li>❌ <strong>Forget contact information</strong>—visitors should be able to follow up</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

### During Your Poster Session

- **Prepare to facilitate discussion:** Bring business cards, paper, sticky notes, and markers
- **Stay at your poster** for the entire session
- **Engage visitors proactively:** *"Would you like me to walk you through it?"*
- **Be ready to discuss** limitations, alternative approaches, and next steps
- **If few people visit:** Use the time to visit other posters, build connections, and observe effective designs

## Accessibility Considerations

Create presentations that work for all attendees:

<div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 p-5 rounded-lg my-6">

- Use **high-contrast color schemes** (avoid light text on light backgrounds)
- **Describe all visual elements** verbally (charts, graphs, images)
- Speak clearly at a **moderate pace**
- Avoid discipline-specific jargon when possible; define necessary terms
- Provide **handouts** for those who want to follow along

</div>

## Troubleshooting

<div class="space-y-4 my-6">
  <details class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 rounded-lg">
    <summary class="p-4 cursor-pointer font-semibold">"My presentation won't load"</summary>
    <div class="p-4 pt-0 text-sm">
      <ul class="space-y-2">
        <li>• This is why you emailed yourself a backup—download from email</li>
        <li>• PDF format is more reliable than PowerPoint if compatibility issues arise</li>
        <li>• Have your 1-page handout ready; you can present from that if necessary</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </details>
  <details class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 rounded-lg">
    <summary class="p-4 cursor-pointer font-semibold">"I'm running out of time"</summary>
    <div class="p-4 pt-0 text-sm">
      <ul class="space-y-2">
        <li>• Skip to your conclusion; summarize findings briefly</li>
        <li>• Offer to discuss details during Q&A or afterward</li>
        <li>• <strong>Never speak faster</strong>—it reduces comprehension</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </details>
  <details class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 rounded-lg">
    <summary class="p-4 cursor-pointer font-semibold">"Someone is challenging my work aggressively"</summary>
    <div class="p-4 pt-0 text-sm">
      <ul class="space-y-2">
        <li>• Listen fully, then acknowledge the valid elements of their critique</li>
        <li>• You do not need to defend every choice in real-time</li>
        <li>• <em>"That is an excellent point I will consider as I revise"</em> is always appropriate</li>
        <li>• Session chairs should redirect hostile questions; make eye contact if you need support</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </details>
  <details class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-850 rounded-lg">
    <summary class="p-4 cursor-pointer font-semibold">"No one is asking questions"</summary>
    <div class="p-4 pt-0 text-sm">
      <ul class="space-y-2">
        <li>• Session chairs typically have prepared questions</li>
        <li>• You can prompt: <em>"One question I have gotten before is..."</em></li>
        <li>• Silence does not mean your work is not interesting—audiences may be processing</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </details>
</div>

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I distribute handouts?**
Yes. Bring 30-40 copies. A 1-page summary with your key contribution, methods, and contact information is most effective.

**What if my co-author is presenting instead of me?**
Either author can present. Coordinate in advance and ensure the presenter can answer methodology questions.

**Are sessions recorded?**
Check the current year's conference policies. If sessions are recorded, presenters will be notified in advance.

**Can I include video or multimedia?**
Yes, but embed all media files in your presentation and test thoroughly beforehand. Have a static backup slide in case of technical issues.

**What's the dress code?**
Smart casual for all events. Conference rooms can vary in temperature; bring layers.
