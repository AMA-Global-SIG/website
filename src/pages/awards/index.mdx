The **AMA Global Marketing SIG** and the **Journal of International Marketing** recognize excellence in international marketing scholarship through four prestigious awards celebrating both sustained career contributions and groundbreaking research.

---

## AMA Global Marketing SIG Awards

The SIG presents two awards annually at the **AMA Global Marketing Conference**, honoring scholars whose work has shaped global marketing theory and practice. Nominations are accepted from the scholarly community — contact the SIG Chair for nomination procedures and deadlines.

### Significant Contributions to Global Marketing Award

Recognizes scholars whose **career-spanning research** has fundamentally advanced international marketing as a discipline.

### Excellence in Global Marketing Research Award

Honors **impactful research contributions** that push the boundaries of global marketing knowledge.

## Journal of International Marketing Awards

The *Journal of International Marketing* Editorial Board presents two awards at the **AMA Summer Educators' Conference**, celebrating published articles that have transformed international marketing scholarship. Awards are selected by the Editorial Board, so no external nominations are required.

### Hans B. Thorelli Award

Honors articles with **sustained, long-term impact** on international marketing theory or practice.

*Awarded for articles published 5+ years prior.*

### S. Tamer Cavusgil Award

Celebrates the article published each calendar year that most effectively **advances international marketing management practice**.
