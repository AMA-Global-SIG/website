## AMA Global Marketing SIG Awards

The SIG presents two awards annually at the **AMA Global Marketing Conference**, honoring scholars whose work has shaped global marketing theory and practice.

## Journal of International Marketing Awards

The *Journal of International Marketing* Editorial Board presents two awards at the **AMA Summer Educators' Conference**, celebrating published articles that have transformed international marketing scholarship.

<div class="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 my-8">
  <Callout class="my-0">
    <h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-heading dark:text-slate-100 mb-3 mt-0">Hans B. Thorelli Award</h3>
    <p class="text-default">Honors articles with <strong>sustained, long-term impact</strong> on international marketing theory or practice.</p>
    <p class="text-sm text-muted mt-3 italic">Awarded for articles published 5+ years prior</p>
  </Callout>
  <Callout class="my-0">
    <h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-heading dark:text-slate-100 mb-3 mt-0">S. Tamer Cavusgil Award</h3>
    <p class="text-default">Celebrates the article published each calendar year that most effectively <strong>advances international marketing management practice</strong>.</p>
  </Callout>
</div>

<Callout>

**Selection Process:** JIM awards are selected by the *Journal of International Marketing* Editorial Board. No external nominations are required.

</Callout>
