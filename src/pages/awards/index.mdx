## Journal of International Marketing Awards

The *Journal of International Marketing* Editorial Board presents two awards at the **AMA Summer Educators' Conference**, celebrating published articles that have transformed international marketing scholarship.

<div class="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 my-8">
  <div class="bg-gradient-to-br from-amber-50 to-amber-100 dark:from-slate-800 dark:to-slate-700 p-6 rounded-xl border border-amber-200 dark:border-slate-600">
    <div class="text-4xl mb-4">📜</div>
    <h3 class="font-bold text-xl mb-3">Hans B. Thorelli Award</h3>
    <p class="text-muted">Honors articles with <strong>sustained, long-term impact</strong> on international marketing theory or practice.</p>
    <p class="text-sm text-muted mt-3 italic">Awarded for articles published 5+ years prior</p>
  </div>
  <div class="bg-gradient-to-br from-amber-50 to-amber-100 dark:from-slate-800 dark:to-slate-700 p-6 rounded-xl border border-amber-200 dark:border-slate-600">
    <div class="text-4xl mb-4">📰</div>
    <h3 class="font-bold text-xl mb-3">S. Tamer Cavusgil Award</h3>
    <p class="text-muted">Celebrates the article published each calendar year that most effectively <strong>advances international marketing management practice</strong>.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="bg-slate-100 dark:bg-slate-800 p-4 rounded-lg my-6">

**Selection Process:** JIM awards are selected by the *Journal of International Marketing* Editorial Board. No external nominations are required.

</div>
