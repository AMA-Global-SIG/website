## Significant Contributions to Global Marketing Award

### What It Recognizes

This award honors scholars whose **career-spanning work** has fundamentally shaped international marketing as a discipline. Recipients have produced influential bodies of research that define how scholars and practitioners understand global markets, cross-cultural consumer behavior, international strategy, and the practice of marketing across borders.

<div class="bg-blue-50 dark:bg-slate-800 p-5 rounded-lg my-6">

**The award celebrates scholars whose publications have:**

- Defined core theoretical frameworks in international marketing
- Generated substantial citation impact across decades
- Influenced how researchers approach global marketing phenomena
- Shaped both academic inquiry and business practice

</div>

### Nomination Process

Nominations are accepted from SIG members and the broader international marketing community.

<div class="bg-slate-100 dark:bg-slate-800 p-5 rounded-lg my-6">

**Required Materials:**

1. **Nomination letter** articulating the candidate's defining contributions
2. **Curriculum vitae**
3. **Publication list** with citation metrics demonstrating long-term impact
4. **Supporting letters** (optional but encouraged)

</div>

**Submission:** Nomination deadlines and procedures are announced annually. Contact the **Vice-Chair of Membership** (listed on the [Leadership & Governance](/leadership) page) for current guidelines.

## Excellence in Global Marketing Research Award

### What It Recognizes

This award celebrates research that advances global marketing knowledge through **innovative approaches**, **rigorous methods**, and **insights that reshape** how we understand international markets.
