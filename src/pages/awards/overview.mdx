The global marketing community recognizes outstanding scholarship through two distinct sets of awards, each presented annually at major AMA conferences.

---

## AMA Global Marketing SIG Awards

The **AMA Global Marketing SIG** presents two awards at the annual **AMA Global Marketing Conference**, honoring individual scholars and impactful research that have advanced the field. Nominations are accepted from the scholarly community; calls open in late fall/early winter with submission deadlines in early spring. A committee of SIG board members and past award recipients reviews nominations. Questions about nominations? Contact the Vice-Chair of Membership listed on our [Leadership & Governance](/leadership) page.

### Significant Contributions to Global Marketing

Honors distinguished scholars who have made sustained, **career-long contributions** to the field of global marketing, recognizing lasting impact on international marketing theory, research, and practice.

[View past recipients →](/awards/significant-contributions)

### Excellence in Global Marketing Research

Recognizes outstanding published research that has made a **significant contribution to the advancement of global marketing knowledge**, demonstrating exceptional scholarly rigor and impact.

## Journal of International Marketing Awards

The **Journal of International Marketing** Editorial Board presents two awards recognizing published articles that have made transformative contributions to international marketing scholarship. Awards are determined through Editorial Board review of published JIM articles; authors do not submit nominations.

### Hans B. Thorelli Award

Recognizes the article that has made the most significant **long-term contribution** to international marketing theory or practice. Re-established as a "five-year-plus" award in 2005.

[View past recipients →](/awards/thorelli-award)

### S. Tamer Cavusgil Award

Established in 1998, honors the article published each calendar year that has made the most significant contribution to **advancing the practice of international marketing management**.
