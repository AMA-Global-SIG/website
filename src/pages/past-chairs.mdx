## Camille P. Schuster

<Callout image="/people/cschuster_215x215.jpg" imageAlt="Camille P. Schuster">

**Professor of Marketing, Cal State San Marcos** | *Chair 1994–1995 (Xavier University) and 2005–2007 (Cal State San Marcos)*

Camille P. Schuster is Professor of Marketing at California State University San Marcos, where she specializes in international marketing, cross-cultural negotiation, and the influence of culture on managerial decision-making. She was a founding member of the Board of Directors of the AMA Global Marketing SIG in 1994 and served as the SIG's first Chair from 1994 to 1995, returning to the role from 2005 to 2007.

Camille's service to the SIG spans more than two decades and nearly every officer position. She has served as International Liaison from 1996 to 2001, Treasurer from 2002 to 2004, Chair-Elect from 2004 to 2005, and SIG historian, in addition to chairing numerous conference events and serving regularly as reviewer, session chair, and discussant. Few members have shaped the SIG's governance, programming, and institutional memory as continuously as she has.

Her research focuses on cross-cultural negotiation, the impact of culture on marketing strategy, and managerial decision-making across borders. She is the author of *Global Business: Planning for Sales and Negotiations*, co-written with Michael Copeland of Procter & Gamble — a work widely regarded as a seminal contribution to cross-cultural negotiation — and *Newer Insights into Cross-Cultural Marketing*, co-authored with Phil Harris of Manchester Metropolitan University. Both volumes have been recognized for translating rigorous research into accessible guidance for managers and educators. Her articles have appeared in the *Journal of International Marketing*, *Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science*, *Journal of Advertising*, *Journal of Public Policy & Marketing*, *Industrial Marketing Management*, *Journal of Global Marketing*, the *International Journal of Customer Relationship Management*, and many other outlets.

Camille is also President of Global Interactions, where she works with corporate clients on cross-cultural strategy and negotiation challenges. Widely recognized in international academic and practitioner communities, she is a frequent speaker and consultant who has conducted training programs, delivered lectures, and consulted with companies in Europe, Australia, Southeast Asia, and Latin America. Across her scholarship, service, and applied work, she has been a unifying figure in the SIG: a founding voice who helped define its early agenda and an ongoing contributor whose institutional knowledge spans the full thirty-year arc of the community.

## David A. Griffith

<Callout image="/people/dagriffith_215x215.jpg" imageAlt="David A. Griffith">

**Hallie Vanderhider Chair in Business and Professor of Marketing, Texas A&M University** | *Chair 2001–2005 (Michigan State University)*

David A. Griffith is the Hallie Vanderhider Chair in Business and Professor of Marketing at the Mays Business School, Texas A&M University. Prior to joining Texas A&M, he held the Iacocca Chair and served as Chair of the Department of Marketing at Lehigh University from 2012 to 2018. Earlier, he served as the John William Byington Endowed Chair in Global Marketing and Director of the Ph.D. Program at Michigan State University, and held faculty appointments at the University of Hawai'i at Mānoa, the Japan-America Institute of Management Science, Wirtschaftsuniversität Wien, and the University of Oklahoma.

David is widely recognized as an authority on marketing strategy, with deep expertise in inter-firm governance, global marketing, and innovation. His research has appeared in the *Journal of Marketing*, *Journal of Marketing Research*, *Journal of International Business Studies*, *Strategic Management Journal*, *Journal of Operations Management*, and many other leading journals. His contributions have been recognized through numerous honors, including the 2019 Significant Contributions to Global Marketing Award and the Excellence in Research Award (2018, 2014) from the AMA Global Marketing SIG, as well as the Hans B. Thorelli Award (2018, 2015) for significant long-term contributions to international marketing. He was ranked first worldwide in publication output (proportional authorship) in international marketing for the 1995–2015 period and was named a "Rising Star" for Economics & Business by Thomson Reuters in 2010.

David served as Editor-in-Chief of the AMA's *Journal of International Marketing* from 2008 to 2013 and as a member of the American Marketing Association's Academic Council from 2014 to 2017. He sits on the editorial review boards of the *Journal of Marketing*, *Journal of International Business Studies*, *Journal of International Marketing*, *Journal of World Business*, and the *Academy of Marketing Science Review*. Through this editorial work he has shaped the field by setting standards for international marketing scholarship and mentoring a generation of authors and reviewers.

His teaching covers marketing analytics, marketing strategy, global marketing, innovation, and international business. He has been recognized with the John D. and Dortha J. Withrow Teacher-Scholar Award at Michigan State University, the Dennis Ching Teaching Excellence Award at the University of Hawai'i at Mānoa, and several additional teaching honors. His service as a past Chair of the AMA Global Marketing SIG continues a long record of leadership across the discipline, combining scholarly distinction, editorial stewardship, and a sustained commitment to community-building within the international marketing field.

## Frank J. Franzak

<Callout image="/people/ffranzak_215x215.jpg" imageAlt="Frank J. Franzak">

**Associate Professor of Marketing, Virginia Commonwealth University** | *Chair 2007–2009 and 2013–2015*

Frank Franzak is Associate Professor of Marketing at the Virginia Commonwealth University School of Business, where he has been on the faculty since 1986 — including a six-year term as Department Chair from July 2002 to July 2008. Prior to joining VCU, he served on the faculty of the Boston College School of Management.

At VCU, Frank serves as Curriculum Coordinator for the Executive MBA program and as Faculty Advisor for the Master of Science in Business with a Global Marketing Management concentration. From 2012 to 2015, he served as Coordinator for the VCU da Vinci Center's Master of Product Innovation program, which connects students from business, design, and engineering around innovation challenges. His applied work focuses on innovation and product launch, often delivered through student-led consulting projects with regional partners.

These engagements have included collaborations with the Virginia Biosciences Commercialization Center and the VCU Office of Technology Transfer on commercialization initiatives, and with the international trade office of the Virginia Economic Development Partnership on global market entry projects. The model reflects his conviction that marketing scholarship is strengthened by sustained dialogue with practice, and his teaching across executive and graduate audiences draws on the same logic — pairing rigorous frameworks with cases drawn directly from his consulting and project work.

His service to the AMA Global Marketing SIG has been central to the community's institutional development. Frank served two terms as Board Chair, from 2007 to 2009 and again from 2013 to 2015, guiding the SIG through a period of growth in conference programming, membership engagement, and international visibility. He continues to serve on the Board as Immediate Past Chair, supporting leadership transitions, mentoring incoming officers, and contributing to long-term strategic planning. Across teaching, research collaboration, and administrative leadership, Frank's career has consistently sat at the intersection of marketing scholarship, executive education, and applied innovation — a trajectory mirrored in his sustained service to the SIG and in the generosity with which he has supported colleagues, doctoral students, and incoming Board members.
