## Camille P. Schuster

<Callout image="/people/cschuster_215x215.jpg" imageAlt="Camille P. Schuster">

**Professor of Marketing, Cal State San Marcos** | *Chair 1994–1995 (Xavier University) and 2005–2007 (Cal State San Marcos)*

Camille P. Schuster is Professor of Marketing at California State University San Marcos, where she specializes in international marketing, cross-cultural negotiation, and the influence of culture on managerial decision-making. A founding member of the Board of Directors of the AMA Global Marketing SIG in 1994, she served as the SIG's first Chair from 1994 to 1995 and returned to the role from 2005 to 2007. She has also held the positions of International Liaison, Treasurer, Chair-Elect, and SIG historian, and has chaired numerous conference events.

Her research focuses on cross-cultural negotiation and the impact of culture on marketing strategy. She is the author of *Global Business: Planning for Sales and Negotiations*, co-written with Michael Copeland of Procter & Gamble — widely regarded as a seminal contribution to cross-cultural negotiation — and *Newer Insights into Cross-Cultural Marketing*, with Phil Harris of Manchester Metropolitan University. Her articles have appeared in the *Journal of International Marketing*, *Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science*, *Journal of Advertising*, *Journal of Public Policy & Marketing*, *Industrial Marketing Management*, *Journal of Global Marketing*, and other outlets. As President of Global Interactions, she also consults internationally, having delivered training and lectures across Europe, Australia, Southeast Asia, and Latin America.

## David A. Griffith

<Callout image="/people/dagriffith_215x215.jpg" imageAlt="David A. Griffith">

**Hallie Vanderhider Chair in Business and Professor of Marketing, Texas A&M University** | *Chair 2001–2005 (Michigan State University)*

David A. Griffith is the Hallie Vanderhider Chair in Business and Professor of Marketing at the Mays Business School, Texas A&M University. He previously held the Iacocca Chair and chaired the Department of Marketing at Lehigh University (2012–2018), and earlier served as the John William Byington Endowed Chair in Global Marketing and Director of the Ph.D. Program at Michigan State University. He has also held appointments at the University of Hawai'i at Mānoa, Wirtschaftsuniversität Wien, and the University of Oklahoma.

David is recognized as a leading authority on marketing strategy, with expertise in inter-firm governance, global marketing, and innovation. His research has appeared in the *Journal of Marketing*, *Journal of Marketing Research*, *Journal of International Business Studies*, *Strategic Management Journal*, and *Journal of Operations Management*, among others. He was ranked first worldwide in international marketing publication output for 1995–2015 and was named a Thomson Reuters "Rising Star" in 2010.

His honors include the 2019 Significant Contributions to Global Marketing Award, the Hans B. Thorelli Award (2018, 2015), and the Excellence in Research Award (2018, 2014) from the AMA Global Marketing SIG. He served as Editor-in-Chief of the *Journal of International Marketing* (2008–2013) and as a member of the AMA's Academic Council (2014–2017), and sits on several editorial review boards across international marketing and management.

## Frank J. Franzak

<Callout image="/people/ffranzak_215x215.jpg" imageAlt="Frank J. Franzak">

**Associate Professor of Marketing, Virginia Commonwealth University** | *Chair 2007–2009 and 2013–2015*

Frank Franzak is Associate Professor of Marketing at the Virginia Commonwealth University School of Business, where he has been on the faculty since 1986 — including six years as Department Chair (2002–2008). Prior to VCU, he served on the faculty of the Boston College School of Management.

At VCU, Frank serves as Curriculum Coordinator for the Executive MBA program and as Faculty Advisor for the Master of Science in Business with a Global Marketing Management concentration. From 2012 to 2015 he coordinated the VCU da Vinci Center's Master of Product Innovation program. His applied work focuses on innovation and product launch, often delivered through student-led consulting projects with the Virginia Biosciences Commercialization Center, the VCU Office of Technology Transfer, and the international trade office of the Virginia Economic Development Partnership.

His service to the AMA Global Marketing SIG has been central to the community's institutional development. Frank served two terms as Board Chair, from 2007 to 2009 and again from 2013 to 2015, and continues on the Board as Immediate Past Chair, supporting leadership transitions and long-term strategic planning.
