### 1. Facilitate Interaction Across Stakeholder Groups

We connect academic researchers, business practitioners, and government representatives to exchange insights on international marketing challenges. This ensures research addresses real-world problems while practitioners access current scholarship.

<div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-650 p-5 rounded-xl border border-brand-200 dark:border-brand-500 shadow-sm my-6">
<h4 class="text-sm font-semibold uppercase tracking-wider text-brand-500 dark:text-brand-300 mb-3">How We Do This</h4>

- **Annual conferences** attract 200–300 participants from 40+ countries for paper presentations, panel discussions, and networking
- We **co-sponsor events** with the Academy of Marketing (UK) and Centers for International Business Education and Research

</div>

---

### 2. Promote Globalization of Marketing Education

We help faculty integrate international perspectives into marketing courses, whether teaching dedicated international marketing classes or adding global dimensions to existing courses.

<div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-650 p-5 rounded-xl border border-brand-200 dark:border-brand-500 shadow-sm my-6">
<h4 class="text-sm font-semibold uppercase tracking-wider text-brand-500 dark:text-brand-300 mb-3">How We Do This</h4>

- **Share teaching resources** including syllabi, case studies, and assignments
- **Connect faculty** with international colleagues for guest lectures, student exchanges, and collaborative teaching projects
- **Provide forums** at conferences for discussing pedagogical approaches

</div>

---

### 3. Foster Research on Global Marketing Issues

We support research on how cultural differences shape marketing strategy, how firms succeed in foreign markets, and how globalization transforms consumer behavior and business practice.

<div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-650 p-5 rounded-xl border border-brand-200 dark:border-brand-500 shadow-sm my-6">
<h4 class="text-sm font-semibold uppercase tracking-wider text-brand-500 dark:text-brand-300 mb-3">How We Do This</h4>

- The annual conference features **competitive paper sessions**, work-in-progress presentations, and a **doctoral consortium** pairing students with senior scholars
- We collaborate with the _Journal of International Marketing_ on **special issues** addressing emerging topics
- Our **500+ member network** connects researchers with shared interests

</div>

---

### 4. Disseminate Knowledge to the Field

We ensure research findings and successful practices reach both academic and practitioner audiences through multiple channels.

<div class="bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-650 p-5 rounded-xl border border-brand-200 dark:border-brand-500 shadow-sm my-6">
<h4 class="text-sm font-semibold uppercase tracking-wider text-brand-500 dark:text-brand-300 mb-3">How We Do This</h4>

- **Present awards** recognizing influential research at AMA conferences
- **Organize special sessions** and tracks at AMA Educators' Conferences
- Work with _JIM_ and other journals to **publish research** that bridges theory and practice
- **Maintain networks** connecting researchers, educators, and practitioners

</div>

## Our Impact

  title="A Global Community Since 1994"

Since 1994, the SIG has become the leading community for international marketing scholarship within the American Marketing Association. Our conferences have launched research collaborations spanning continents, our doctoral consortium has mentored generations of scholars now leading the field, and our awards recognize research that has fundamentally shaped how we understand global markets.

The SIG's members represent the breadth and depth of international marketing scholarship—from consumer behavior researchers studying cultural differences to strategy scholars examining market entry decisions to practitioners implementing global marketing programs.
