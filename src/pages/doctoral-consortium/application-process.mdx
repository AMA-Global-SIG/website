The AMA Global Marketing SIG Doctoral Consortium provides a unique opportunity for PhD students with an interest in international and cross-cultural marketing to advance their research and careers.

---

## Who Should Apply

<div class="bg-blue-50 dark:bg-slate-800 p-6 rounded-lg border-l-4 border-blue-500 my-6">

The Consortium welcomes **doctoral students in marketing or international business** who:

- Have a research focus on **international marketing**, **cross-cultural marketing**, or **global business**
- Have begun developing their dissertation research (students at any stage with well-defined research ideas are welcome)
- Seek to build connections within the international marketing academic community

</div>

---

## Application Requirements

Applications should be submitted through the **EasyChair** conference system and must include:

<div class="space-y-4 my-6">
  <div class="flex gap-4 p-5 bg-slate-100 dark:bg-slate-800 rounded-lg">
    <span class="flex-shrink-0 w-8 h-8 bg-blue-600 text-white rounded-full flex items-center justify-center font-bold">1</span>
    <div>
      <h4 class="font-bold">Cover Letter</h4>
      <p class="text-sm text-muted">Describing your stage in the doctoral program, research interests, what you hope to gain from participating, and whether you wish to be considered for a travel stipend.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="flex gap-4 p-5 bg-slate-100 dark:bg-slate-800 rounded-lg">
    <span class="flex-shrink-0 w-8 h-8 bg-blue-600 text-white rounded-full flex items-center justify-center font-bold">2</span>
    <div>
      <h4 class="font-bold">Research Abstract</h4>
      <p class="text-sm text-muted mb-2">A current research project focused on international marketing (maximum 5 pages, single-spaced). Include:</p>
      <ul class="text-sm text-muted list-disc list-inside space-y-1">
        <li>Title and keywords</li>
        <li>Research question and motivation</li>
        <li>Theoretical background and intended contribution</li>
        <li>Proposed methodology or research design</li>
        <li>Expected findings or preliminary results (if available)</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="flex gap-4 p-5 bg-slate-100 dark:bg-slate-800 rounded-lg">
    <span class="flex-shrink-0 w-8 h-8 bg-blue-600 text-white rounded-full flex items-center justify-center font-bold">3</span>
    <div>
      <h4 class="font-bold">Curriculum Vitae</h4>
      <p class="text-sm text-muted">Maximum 2 pages including education, research experience, publications or working papers, and conference presentations.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<Note
  title="Submission Format"
  description="Please combine all materials into a single PDF file for submission. If you have also submitted a paper to the main conference, indicate the title and track in your cover letter."
/>

## Travel Stipends

<div class="bg-green-50 dark:bg-green-900/20 border-l-4 border-green-500 p-5 rounded-r-lg my-6">

A limited number of **travel stipends** are available to help offset participation costs. Stipend amounts and availability vary by year.

**To be considered:** Indicate your interest and briefly describe your need for financial support in your cover letter.

</div>

## What to Expect

The Consortium takes place on the **day preceding the main conference**. The program typically includes:

<div class="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-4 my-6">
  <div class="p-5 bg-blue-50 dark:bg-slate-800 rounded-lg">
    <h4 class="font-bold mb-2">Panel Discussions</h4>
    <p class="text-sm text-muted">Senior faculty share insights on research trends, publishing strategies, and career development.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="p-5 bg-blue-50 dark:bg-slate-800 rounded-lg">
    <h4 class="font-bold mb-2">Small Group Sessions</h4>
    <p class="text-sm text-muted">Present your research and receive detailed feedback from faculty mentors.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="p-5 bg-blue-50 dark:bg-slate-800 rounded-lg">
    <h4 class="font-bold mb-2">Meet-the-Editors Sessions</h4>
    <p class="text-sm text-muted">Connect with representatives from leading international marketing journals.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="p-5 bg-blue-50 dark:bg-slate-800 rounded-lg">
    <h4 class="font-bold mb-2">Networking Reception</h4>
    <p class="text-sm text-muted">Build relationships with faculty and fellow doctoral students.</p>
  </div>
</div>
