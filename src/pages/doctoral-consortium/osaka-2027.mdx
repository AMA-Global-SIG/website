Held annually in conjunction with the AMA Global Marketing Conference, the Consortium brings together 15–25 doctoral students from around the world with leading scholars in the field. ## Eligibility & Application Requirements

The Consortium welcomes doctoral students in marketing or international business who have a research focus on international marketing, cross-cultural marketing, or global business; who have begun developing their dissertation research; and who seek to build connections within the international marketing academic community. Students at any stage are welcome, provided they have a well-defined research idea.

Applications are submitted through the **EasyChair** conference system as a single combined PDF and must include:

- **Cover Letter:** Describe your stage in the doctoral program, your research interests, what you hope to gain from participating, and whether you wish to be considered for a travel stipend.
- **Research Abstract** (max 5 pages, single-spaced): A current research project focused on international marketing, covering your research question and motivation, theoretical background and intended contribution, proposed methodology or research design, and expected findings or preliminary results (if available). Include a title and keywords.
- **Curriculum Vitae** (max 2 pages): Include education, research experience, publications or working papers, and conference presentations.

---

## Selection & Travel Stipends

Applications are evaluated holistically, with particular attention to topic fit (research relating to global, international, or cross-cultural marketing), the strength and clarity of the intended theoretical or practical contribution, and the overall coherence and quality of the submitted abstract. The selection committee strives to assemble a diverse cohort representing a range of research topics, methodological approaches, and institutional backgrounds.

A limited number of **travel stipends** are available to help offset participation costs. Stipend amounts and availability vary by year. To be considered, indicate your interest and briefly describe your need for financial support in your cover letter.
