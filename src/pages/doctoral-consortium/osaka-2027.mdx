## About the 2027 Osaka Consortium

The AMA Global Marketing SIG invites applications for its **2027 Doctoral Consortium**, a workshop designed for PhD students specializing in international and cross-cultural marketing. The Consortium aims to foster the next generation of scholars by providing an enriching environment to develop research ideas, navigate the complexities of international marketing research, and establish the foundation for a successful academic career. The event takes place on the day preceding the main conference and brings together 15–25 doctoral students with leading scholars in the field.

---

## Objectives

The Consortium facilitates in-depth discussions between doctoral students and distinguished faculty on the topics that shape an early academic career:

- Emerging trends in international and cross-cultural marketing research
- Challenges of conducting high-impact research for top-tier journals
- Best practices for managing dissertation work and the academic job market
- Essentials for kick-starting an academic career

---

## Format

The Consortium offers an intimate, collaborative atmosphere for direct engagement with established leaders in the field. Past mentors have included current and former editors from premier journals such as the *Journal of Marketing*, *Journal of Marketing Research*, *Journal of International Marketing*, and *Journal of International Business Studies*.

## Eligibility & Application Requirements

The Consortium welcomes doctoral students in marketing or international business who have a research focus on international, cross-cultural, or global marketing and who are actively shaping their dissertation research agenda. Students at all stages of the doctoral program are welcome, provided they have a well-defined research idea.

Applications are submitted through the **EasyChair** conference system as a single combined PDF and must include:

- **Cover Letter:** State your stage in the doctoral program, your research interests, what you hope to gain from participating, and whether you wish to be considered for a travel stipend.
- **Extended Abstract** (max 5 pages): A research project focused on international marketing. Begin with title, author information, abstract, and keywords, then cover the research question and motivation, theoretical background, proposed methodology or research design, and intended contribution. Place diagrams, tables, and references in appendices at the end.
- **Curriculum Vitae** (max 2 pages): Education, research experience, publications or working papers, and conference presentations.

**Optional Enhancement.** To maximize the feedback you receive, you may submit a more complete paper or presentation deck by a later pre-review deadline (announced with the call for applications). This material is shared with the committee in advance of the Consortium.

---

## Selection & Travel Stipends

Applications are evaluated holistically, with particular attention to topic fit (research relating to global, international, or cross-cultural marketing), the strength and clarity of the intended contribution, and the overall coherence of the submitted abstract. The selection committee strives to assemble a diverse cohort representing a range of research topics, methodological approaches, and institutional backgrounds.

The AMA Global Marketing SIG provides a limited number of **travel stipends** based on individual funding need. To be considered, indicate your need clearly in your cover letter. Stipends are typically disbursed upon the student's presentation at the Consortium and may be applied toward travel expenses and/or conference registration fees.
