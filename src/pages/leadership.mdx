## Board Structure

The **AMA Global Marketing SIG** is governed by a **Board of Directors** composed of accomplished scholars who provide strategic guidance, organize conferences, develop programs, and support the SIG's mission. Board members typically serve three-year terms and represent diverse geographic regions, research specializations, and institutional types.

## Board Member Responsibilities

Board members contribute to SIG governance and operations through specific roles. The following outlines key responsibilities for each officer position.

### Chairperson

<Callout title="Primary Leadership Role">

- Coordinate with the AMA on policies and guidelines of SIG operations
- Ensure that the SIG operates within the guidelines as set forth by the AMA
- Lead strategic initiatives, inclusive of budgeting and planning of activities
- Complete required AMA reports (e.g., budget requests, SIG activities)
- Advise and coordinate the activities of other SIG officers
- Chair Board meetings at Winter and Summer AMA Educators' Conferences
- Write quarterly "message from the Chair" for inclusion in SIG communications
- Keep in contact with other officers throughout the year

</Callout>

### Chair-Elect / Immediate Past Chair

<Callout title="Continuity & Transition">

- Assume responsibilities of the Chairperson at request or when Chairperson is absent
- Become familiar with current Chairperson activities through review of prior reports
- Prepare annual planning documents required by the AMA
- Work with the AMA website administrator to facilitate distribution of Global Marketing material

</Callout>

### Treasurer / Financial Officer

<Callout title="Financial Oversight">

- Ensure compliance with AMA guidelines regarding SIG expenses
- Coordinate with the AMA for reimbursement of SIG expenses
- Enter all receipts and expenses in account book and/or computer record
- Prepare annual fiscal report for the Chair
- Promote relationships with external groups for development purposes
- Take minutes of each meeting, including motions and votes
- Distribute minutes of each meeting to SIG officers

</Callout>

### Vice-Chair of Communications

<Callout title="Member Communications">

- Communicate with the Global Marketing SIG membership
- Update and maintain email and mailing lists
- Work with the SIG Webmaster to post news on the website
- Work with the AMA website administrator to facilitate distribution of materials
- Promote the SIG within the AMA

</Callout>

### Vice-Chair of Membership / Academic Advisor

<Callout title="Membership & Recognition">

- Promote the SIG within the AMA and initiate recruitment efforts
- Work with membership to ensure the SIG is meeting their needs
- Coordinate solicitation of nominations for SIG awards from the membership
- Form a committee of SIG officers to serve on the Awards and Recognition committee
- Secure appropriate awards for recipients (e.g., plaques)
- Work with the Treasurer on sources of funding for additional awards

</Callout>

### International Liaison

<Callout title="Global Outreach">

- Facilitate coordination of activities with non-U.S. marketing and/or international business groups
- Represent the interests of non-U.S. based SIG members
- Build awareness and interest of non-U.S. academics regarding conference events
- Stimulate cooperative relationships between non-U.S. academics and Global Marketing SIG members

</Callout>

### Conference Liaison

<Callout title="Event Coordination">

- Plan annual membership meeting (summer AMA) and membership reception (winter AMA)
- Build awareness and interest of membership regarding conference events
- Coordinate activities with the Global Marketing track chair for each conference
- Stimulate conference attendees to attend various SIG activities
- Update website with current information concerning AMA conference events

</Callout>

### Webmaster / Social Media Communication

<Callout title="Digital Presence">

- Maintain and update SIG website with current information
- Work with Vice-Chair of Communications to post news and announcements
- Coordinate with Conference Liaison for posting conference-related information
- Manage social media channels and digital communications
- Ensure website content accuracy and accessibility

</Callout>

### Doctoral Student Liaison / PhD Liaison

<Callout title="Doctoral Support">

- Establish and maintain relationship with the AMA DocSIG
- Ensure that AMA DocSIG members receive Global Marketing SIG communications
- Work with doctoral students to propose special conference events
- Coordinate doctoral consortium activities

</Callout>

### Past-Chair

<Callout title="Advisory & Support">

- Assist the Chair in assuming responsibilities as SIG Chair
- Facilitate smooth transition of leadership in the SIG and between the SIG and the AMA
- Assist the Vice-Chair of Membership with recruitment efforts
- Assist the Treasurer with development efforts

</Callout>
