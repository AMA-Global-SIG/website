## Board Structure

The **AMA Global Marketing SIG** is governed by a **Board of Directors** composed of accomplished scholars who provide strategic guidance, organize conferences, develop programs, and support the SIG's mission. Board members typically serve three-year terms and represent diverse geographic regions, research specializations, and institutional types.

The Board meets at least twice annually during AMA conferences and communicates regularly throughout the year on SIG initiatives, programming, and strategic priorities. Specific responsibilities are distributed across the leadership transition roles (Chair, Chair-Elect, Past-Chair), a set of officers, and several liaisons.

---

## Board Member Roles

### Chair

Provides overall strategic direction and ensures the SIG operates in alignment with AMA policies. The Chair leads budgeting and planning, coordinates the activities of other officers, chairs Board meetings at the Winter and Summer AMA Educators' Conferences, and authors the quarterly message to members.

### Chair-Elect

Prepares to assume the chairmanship by shadowing the current Chair, reviewing prior reports, and preparing the annual planning documents required by the AMA. Steps in for the Chair when needed and helps coordinate distribution of Global Marketing materials with the AMA website administrator.

### Past-Chair

Supports the incoming Chair through the leadership transition and serves as an advisor on the SIG's relationship with the AMA. Also assists the Vice-Chair of Membership with recruitment and the Treasurer with development efforts.

### Treasurer / Financial Officer

Manages SIG finances in line with AMA guidelines — tracking receipts and expenses, coordinating reimbursements with the AMA, and preparing the annual fiscal report. Also cultivates external development relationships and records and distributes the minutes of each Board meeting.

### Vice-Chair of Communications

Leads communication with the SIG membership — maintaining email and mailing lists, coordinating with the Webmaster and the AMA website administrator on news and announcements, and promoting the SIG within the broader AMA.

### Webmaster / Social Media Communication

Maintains the SIG website and social media presence — keeping content current and accessible, coordinating with the Vice-Chair of Communications on news, and posting conference-related information in collaboration with the Conference Liaison.

### Vice-Chair of Membership / Academic Advisor

Drives membership recruitment, gathers feedback to ensure the SIG meets members' needs, and oversees the SIG's awards programs — coordinating nominations, convening the Awards and Recognition committee, and working with the Treasurer on award funding.

### Conference Liaison

Plans the annual membership meeting at the summer AMA and the membership reception at the winter AMA, coordinates with the Global Marketing track chair for each conference, and promotes SIG activities to conference attendees.

### International Liaison

Represents the interests of non-U.S.-based SIG members and coordinates with international marketing and business organizations abroad. Builds conference awareness among non-U.S. academics and fosters cooperative relationships across borders.

### Doctoral Student Liaison

Coordinates the SIG's doctoral consortium and serves as a bridge to the AMA DocSIG — ensuring DocSIG members receive Global Marketing SIG communications and helping doctoral students propose special conference events.

## Bylaws

The SIG's operations, membership, and leadership are governed by formal bylaws covering the group's focus and goals, membership, the Board of Directors and officers, sample activities, finances, and the procedure for amending the bylaws themselves.
