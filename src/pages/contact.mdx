### Affiliated Membership Application

**Required Materials:**

1. Current position and institutional affiliation
2. Brief statement of research interests in international marketing
3. CV or brief biographical summary
4. Name of current SIG member who supports your application (if applicable)

**To Apply:** Email completed materials to the Membership Coordinator

> **Subject line:** "Affiliated Membership Application - [Your Name]"

**Membership Terms:** Affiliated membership is granted for one-year renewable periods beginning in August, subject to Board of Directors approval.
